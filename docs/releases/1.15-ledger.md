## Non-claims and inactive channels

Syntax, AST, mutation, coverage, and proof evidence remain advisory unless a
downstream policy promotes them. This release makes no UB-absence claim and no
automatic merge verdict. The inactive Homebrew, AUR, Scoop, and WinGet
templates are removed from the supported release surface rather than updated
with unverifiable hashes.

## Post-release closeout

After stable verification, record GitHub assets, checksums, attestations,
representative crates, Cargo install, Nix, Action binary/container, GHCR exact
and mutable tags, and browser/WASM results. Then restore `## [Unreleased]`,
update `NOW`, `NEXT`, and `ROADMAP`, and leave this ledger as the durable
release receipt.
