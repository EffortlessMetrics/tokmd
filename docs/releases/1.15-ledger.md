## Preflight boundary

The release workflow and hosted consumer matrix are the authoritative
artifact evidence. The isolated local full workspace test and
`cargo xtask publish --dry-run` attempts were stopped by concurrent Windows
Cargo workload during package validation and remain `not proven`; they are
not represented as passes.

## Non-claims and inactive channels

Syntax, AST, mutation, coverage, and proof evidence remain advisory unless a
downstream policy promotes them. This release makes no UB-absence claim and no
automatic merge verdict. Homebrew, AUR, Scoop, and WinGet prototypes are not
supported 1.15 distribution channels.
